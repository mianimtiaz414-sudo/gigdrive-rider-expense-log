Privacy Policy for GigDrive

Last updated: November 4, 2026

GigDrive ("we", "our", or "us") operates the GigDrive mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

GigDrive is primarily an offline utility application designed to help riders, delivery drivers, and gig workers log earnings, fuel expenses, repairs, maintenance, and mileage.

  • Local Storage: All financial logs, cash logs, and expense entries you record are stored locally on your mobile device using encrypted or structured local database systems.
  • Cloud Authentication & Sync: If you choose to log in using an email account or Google Sign-In, your credentials and a secure backup of your expense logs are securely synchronized with Google Firebase Cloud Services. This is strictly to allow you to restore your data or switch accounts seamlessly.

2. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or rent out your personal data or financial logs to any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or external organizations. Your logged financial details are completely private to you.

3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes Google Firebase for user authentication, cloud syncing, and secure backup infrastructure. These services process data securely in alignment with Google's strict security protocols.

4. Data Retention and Security

Your local data stays on your device until you clear the application storage or uninstall the App. If you use cloud sync, your data is retained securely on cloud servers. We provide robust options inside the App's account controls to "Logout" or "Switch Account," which safely backs up any pending local logs and fully clears the local device cache to guarantee absolute cross-account data protection.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy document on this official host page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@gigdriveapp.com
